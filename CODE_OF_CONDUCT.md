# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free experience
for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender
identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance,
race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our Standards

Examples of positive behavior include demonstrating empathy, giving constructive
feedback, accepting responsibility, and focusing on what is best for the
community. Harassment, personal attacks, and publication of private information
without permission are unacceptable.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior through GitHub's private reporting mechanisms or
the repository security contact. Maintainers may remove, edit, reject, or ban
contributions that do not align with this Code of Conduct.

This policy is adapted from Contributor Covenant 2.1.
